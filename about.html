About Sahara Computers & Education Institute

Sahara Computers & Education Institute is a computer and education training institute in G B Palya, Bangalore. We are ISO Certified 9001:2015. We provide training in many computer and professional courses so students can learn useful skills for study and work.

We offer different types of courses. Students can learn computer basics like MS Word, MS Office and typing. We also teach software and programming courses such as C, C++, Java, HTML, SQL and Tally.

For students who like design, we have graphic design courses like CorelDraw, Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign. We also provide hardware and networking courses, and AutoCAD for drawing and design.

Students can choose short courses on a single subject, or longer professional courses that cover many topics. Every course is made simple and practical so students can learn step by step.
