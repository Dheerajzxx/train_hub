Buttons: - Primary: rounded blue - Secondary: outline

Cards: - radius 18px - shadow - hover lift

Spacing: 80px between sections.

Animations: Fade-up. Zoom. Counter. Slider.

Icons: Font Awesome.

Images: Rounded 24px.
